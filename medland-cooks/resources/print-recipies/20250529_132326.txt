Jelly Roll

3 eggs
1/2 cup fine granulated sugar
3/4 cup all-purpose flour
I tablespoon hot water
little sugar
wax paper

Combine eggs and sugar in a
large mixing bowl, beat until the
mixture is thick and creamy. Sift
flour and fold gently into the egg
mixture with a metal spoon and
then fold in the water. Pour into a
lined jelly roll pan 9 by 12 inches.
Bake 7 to 10 minutes on the top
rack in 400 degree oven. Test cake
before removing from oven — press
with finger, if no impression
remains the cake is done. Turn onto
sugared paper, spread with jam, roll
firmly and dredge with sugar.
Makes 8 servings when sliced.
