SWEET DILLS
Make syrup of:
4 cups sugar
1 quart vinegar
1 pint water
12 cup salt
'/4 teaspoon celery seed

Wash and put pickles in jars. Put a
sprig of dill and two slices of onion
in bottom of each jar. On top of
pickles pour boiling Syrup over
pickles. These are ready to use in
four weeks.
