1 young goose 4 cups poultry stuffing seasoned
with onion and sage.
Lightly stuff goose. Truss legs and wings to lie close to the
body. Roast at 325° F. for 3 to 4 hours, basting occasionally with
pan juices. Simmer giblets until tender.
Prepare gravy by pouring off most of the fat and adding
some brown flour, the cooked, chopped giblets, and a little pars-
Jey. Use cooking broth from giblets for liquid. To brown flour,
spread on a pie plate and set in a very hot oven. When it begins
to color, stir until it is uniformly brown. Store in a tightly closed
container.
