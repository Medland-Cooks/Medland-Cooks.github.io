1 cup sugar

1 tsp. Schilling Cinnamon

I/ cup milk

1 tsp. Schilling pure Vanilla Extract

3 cups walnut or pecan halves
Combine sugar, cinnamon and milk; cook to 236°F. (soft ball stage). Add vanilla
and walnuts. Stir mixture until thick and grainy. Immediately turn out on waxed
paper and separate the nuts, working quickly.
