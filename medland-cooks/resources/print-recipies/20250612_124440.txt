Make, freeze dough up to month before baking:

1. Sift flour with baking powder and salt. In
large bowl, with electric mixer at high speed,
cream shortening well; now, at medium
speed, add granulated and brown sugars and
continue beating until well blended. Beat in
unbeaten eggs and vanilla until well blended.

2. Now, at low speed, add half of flour mix-
ture, then milk, then rest of flour.

3. Place dough in freezer container; freezer-
wrap and freeze up to a month.
