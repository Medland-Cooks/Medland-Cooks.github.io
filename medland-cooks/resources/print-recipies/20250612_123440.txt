CHRISTMAS CHORISTERS

Make any time up to one month ahead:

1. Lay a dried tree hranch, 20” by 16”, on wayx-
paper-covered board or counter. In large
bowl, with electric mixer at high speed,
beat 4 egg whites until soft peaks form.
Then add 4% cups sifted confectioners’
sugar, 1 cup at a time, beating thoroughly
after each addition; continue beating about
5 minutes.

2. With tip of small knife or spatula, spread
icing generously over branch, letting it drip
off ends and undersides in “pointed drips.”
Let stand overnight until dry and hard, then
gently loosen from paper. With brush or fin-
gertips, lightly coat top of branch with
white corn syrup; then sprinkle coarsely-
ground salt (kosher salt) over syrup for a
snowy effect. Gently transfer branch to a
tray, or piece of foil-covered cardboard.

3. Now tear about 2 feet of saran from a 12-
inch roll; fold it in half lengthwise, then
fold it in half lengthwise again. Wind this
strip of saran around your fingers, then
shape it into a 3-inch “nest,” securing end
with cellophane tape. Place nest on branch
where it will be firmly supported.

4. Then, with a bit of glue, or by other means
if your hirds* have their own attaching de-
vice, fasten them to edge of nest as pic-
tured. If desired, let one little bird stray to
an adjoining branch to watch the fun! Place
centerpiece on dining table, surrounded with
slim pastel candles to be lighted for Christ-
mas dinner. ¢
