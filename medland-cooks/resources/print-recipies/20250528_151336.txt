Walnut crescents Yield: 4 dozen cookies
1 cup sifted all-purpose flour 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1/8 teaspoon salt 1/2 cup finely ground walnuts
1/2 cup butter, softened 1-2 teaspoons milk, if necessary
1/2 cup granulated sugar 1 cup confectioners’ sugar
Preheat oven to 300°F. Sift flour and salt onto a sheet of waxed paper. Combine
butter, sugar, and vanilla extract in a large mixing bow!. Cream on Speed 7 for 1
minute. Add walnuts and mix until well blended. Add milk, if necessary. Using
level teaspoons of dough, roll into crescent shapes and place on ungreased cookie
sheet. Bake for 20-25 minutes. Remove from cookie sheet and roll in confec-
tioners’ sugar. Cool on wire racks.
