DEVILED EGGSv

8 hard-cooked eggs

1 tbsp. lemon juice or vinegar

14 to 1 tsp. prepared yellow
mustard

15 tsp. salt ‘

1 tsp. Worcestershire sauce

14 tsp. pepper _

3 tbsp. mayonnaise

Paprika

Cut the eggs in half and remove the

yolks. Mash the yolks with a fork or

press them through a sieve. Combine

‘with the lemon juice, mustard, salt,

Worcestershire sauce, pepper and

mayonnaise, and beat until smooth.

Refill the whites with the mixture,

using a spoon or piping it in with a

pastry bag. Sprinkle paprika over the

yolk mixture in each egg half.
