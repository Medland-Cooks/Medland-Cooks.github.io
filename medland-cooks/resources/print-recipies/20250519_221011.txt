BROWNIES’ BROWNIES
15 ¢. butter
3 oz. (4 squares) unsweetened
chocolate
2 ¢. sugar '
1 tsp. salt
' 4 eggs / |
1 ¢. flour |
1 tsp. pure vanilla extract
1 c. chopped nuts, preferably
walnuts.
Preheat the oven to 375 degrees.
Butter a 10-by-14-inch pan. Melt
the butter and chocolate gently in
a saucepan. Add the sugar and salt,
stirring. Remove from the heat.
‘Beat in the eggs one at a time and
stir in the flour, vanilla and nuts.
‘Pour and scrape the mixture into
‘the pan and bake 20 to 25 minutes.
Cut while hot. -
Yield: 30 to 50 brownies, depend-
_ing on how you cut them.
