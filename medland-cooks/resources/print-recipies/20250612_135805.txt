2. Meanwhile, in 1-quart saucepan, mix 2 tea-
spoons cornstarch, 2 teaspoon nutmeg, 2
teaspoons salt, 2 cup granulated sugar,
Gradually stir in 1 cup water; cook over low
heat, while stirring, until clear sauce. Stir
in 1 tablespoon lemon juice, 3 cup brandy.

3. Start heating oven to 375°F. Peel potatoes;
slice crosswise, Y4-to-Y2-inch thick, into
buttered shallow baking dish, Pour on sauce;
then bake, covered, 30 minutes or until
glazed, basting occasionally.

4. When glazed, sprinkle a few miniature
marshmallows over potatoes, as pictured;
broil just until golden. Makes about 8 serv-
ings.
