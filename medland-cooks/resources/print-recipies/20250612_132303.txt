CHILI

Va cup olive oil

2 large cloves garlic, peeled, minced

4 large yellow onions, peeled, chopped

2 large green peppers, seeded, minced

4 pounds lean chuck, ground

3 or 4 1-pound, 3-ounce cans tomatoes

2 6-ounce cans tomato paste

Ya to V3 cup chili powder

1 teaspoon white vinegar

3 dashes cayenne pepper

1 tablespoon salt

4 16-ounce cans red kidney beans

3 whole cloves

1 bay leaf

Make, then freeze up to one month ahead:

1. In 8-quart Dutch oven heat oil; in it sauté
garlic, onions, peppers until tender. Add
chuck, crumbling it loosely; cook slowly,
over medium heat, 10 minutes or until chuck
loses red color.

2. Add tomatoes, cutting large chunks of tomato
smaller with two knives; add tomato paste,
chili powder, vinegar, cayenne and salt.

3. Stir in kidney beans, cloves and bay leaf;
then cook, uncovered, 1 hour. At end of
hour, check liquid content; if there's too
much, simmer a bit longer; if too dry, add
another can of tomatoes.

4. Refrigerate chili mixture till cool. Then turn
it into freezer-type plastic bags or contain-
ers and freeze up to 1 month. To serve, re-
move desired amount from freezer; turn
into a Dutch oven and heat, over very low
heat, until hot and bubbly. Serve in bowls
(about 34 cup per serving), for brunch. In-
crease to 1-cup servings, if to be a main
dish at lunch or supper. Makes 8 quarts or
about 32 1-cup servings.
