2. On foil-covered pastry board, carefully stand
3 tree branches, on their bases, with sides

fit perfectly, fill in and shape into a rounded
point with icing.

3. Now, with icing, lay about 15 red and white
gumdrop “fringes,” one above the other,
over each of 3 side seams, starting at bot-
tom with a red “fringe.” If needed, place
one or two rows of “fringes” around top of
tree to cover areas you filled in with _icing.
