Quick egg white frosting vield: Generously fills and frosts 2 (8 or 9-inch)
cake layers
3/4 cup light corn syrup 1/4 cup granulated sugar
2 egg whites 1 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
dash of salt
Heat corn syrup in small saucepan to a boil. In small mixing bowl, beat egg whites
and salt on Speed 12 for 30 seconds—until foamy. Gradually add sugar, continu-
ing to beat until stiff peaks form—about 1 minute. Slowly add boiling corn syrup to
egg white mixture. Add vanilla extract. Scrape sides of bow| as necessary. Con-
tinue beating until frosting becomes thick enough to spread-about 3 minutes.
Spread onto cake in swirled pattern. Serve immediately.
Vanilla glaze Yield: 1/2 cup glaze
1 cup confectioners’ sugar 1 teaspoon light corn syrup
2 tablespoons milk food coloring, if desired
1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
In a small mixing bowl!, combine confectioners’ sugar, milk, and vanilla extract.
Moisten ingredients on Speed 2 for 1 minute. Add corn syrup and food coloring.
Blend on Speed 7 for 1 more minute. Scrape sides of bow| as necessary. Drizzle
onto cookies or cake.
Whipped cream topping Yield: Fills and frosts 2 (8-inch) cake layers
1/2 pint whipping cream 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1 tablespoon confectioners’ sugar
Chill whipping cream, beaters, and small mixing bowl. Pour cream into chilled
bowl. Beat on Speed 9. Gradually add sugar and vanilla extract. Continue beating
until soft, glossy peaks form. Do not overbeat! Scrape sides of bowl as necessary.
Spread onto cake. Cover and chill before serving.
