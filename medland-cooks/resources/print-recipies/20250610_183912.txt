ADELAIDES

1 cup brown sugar

1 cup white sugar

1 cup shortening

2 eggs, beaten

3 cups sifted flour

114 tsps. soda

1/ tsp. salt

2 tsps. vanilla

1 cup dates, chopped

1 cup chopped walnuts

Cream together the sugars and shortening.
Beat in the eggs. Sift the flour with the soda
and salt. Stir in. Beat until blended. Add vanilla.
Stir in dates and nuts. This batter will be so
thick that it may be necessary to do the final
blending by hand. Form in large flattened balls.
Place on greased cookie sheet three inches
apart. Bake at 375° for 12-15 minutes. When
baked, roll each ball in granulated sugar.
