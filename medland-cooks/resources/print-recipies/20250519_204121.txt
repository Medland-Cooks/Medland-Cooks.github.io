GINGERBREAD

15, c. shortening

14 c. sugar

1 c. molasses

1 tsp. ginger

14 tsp. salt

1 beaten egg |

1 tsp. soda

1 c. boiling water

2 c. flour |

Cream shortening and sugar.

Add molasses, ginger, salt and |

egg. Mix in soda that has been

dissolved in boiling water. Add

flour and mix. Put in a greased

jelly-roll pan and bake 25 min-

utes in a moderate oven (350

degrees).

APPLE PIE

6 or 7 tart apples

1 c. sugar

2 tbsp. flour

1 tsp. cinnamon

Dash of salt

Pastry for 2-crust pie

2 tbsp. butter

Peel and slice apples. Mix with

sugar, flour, cinnamon and

salt. Pour into unbaked pastry
| shell, dot with butter and cover
| with top pastry. Flute edges

~and prick top to allow steam to
escape. Bake in a hot oven
(400 degrees) for 50 minutes.
