DATE NUT BREAD
1 small pkg. dates (14 1b.),
cut in half

1 cup boiling water

1/ tsp. soda

1 cup dark brown sugar

1 egg

1 cup graham flour

1 cup white flour

14 tsp. baking powder

1 tsp. salt

1, cup melted shortening

1, cup chopped walnuts

Pour boiling water over dates and stir. Add
soda. Cool. Add brown sugar and unbeaten egg
and beat mixture. Sift together flour, baking
powder, and salt. Stir into mixture. Stir in
melted shortening. Fold in walnuts. Pour into
greased loaf pan, 8 x 4 or into two smaller pans,
6145 x 3. Bake for one hour at 325°.
