1 to 3 tablespoons hot water, or as

Combine sugar, cornstarch, flavorings and
rum or brandy. Gradually stir in hot water
a tablespoonful at a time, until you have
a smooth thick paste. Spread on warm
- cookies. If mixture cools and thickens as
