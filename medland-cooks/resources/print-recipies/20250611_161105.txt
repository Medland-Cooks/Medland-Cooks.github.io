Mix sugar, cornstarch, and salt in
the top of a double boiler. Blend in
milk gradually. Cook over boiling
water, stirring occasionally, until thick-
ened, about 12 to 15 minutes. Stir
a small amount of the hot cornstarch
mixture into the beaten eggs; then stix
the eggs into the remaining cornstarch
mixture. Cook over boiling water 4 to
5 minutes longer, stirring constantly.
Chill thoroughly. (This is essential for
