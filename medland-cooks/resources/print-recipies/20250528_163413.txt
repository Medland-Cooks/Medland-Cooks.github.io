NUTTY PEACH NESTSv

6 well-drained canned peach halves
14 ¢. chopped peanuts

14 c. shredded coconut

14 c. firmly packed brown sugar -

14 c. orange juice

30 miniature marshmallows

Grease a shallow baking dish. Place
peaches cut side up in dish. Sprinkle
with chopped peanuts and coconut.
Combine brown sugar and orange
juice. Pour over peaches.

Place 5 marshmallows to resemble
€ggs on each peach half. Bake in a
moderate oven (350 degrees) 15
minutes or until marshmallows are
golden on top. Serve warm or
chilted, spooning sauce from bottom
of baking dish over the top of each
peach. Makes 6 servings.
