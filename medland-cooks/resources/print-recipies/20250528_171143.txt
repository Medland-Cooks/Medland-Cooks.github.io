Butterscotch Haystacks
One 6-0z. pkg. (1 cup) Nestlé Butterscotch Morsels
1 cup sugar-coated cereal flakes
% cup shredded coconut
Melt over hot (not boiling) water, Nestlé Butterscotch
Morsels. Remove from heat. Stir in cereal flakes and
shredded coconut; toss gently until well coated. Drop
by measuring teaspoonfuls onto waxed paper-lined
cookie sheets. Chill in refrigerator until set (about 30
minutes).

Makes: 272 dozen
