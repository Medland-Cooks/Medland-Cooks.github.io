QUAKERS

2 cups brown sugar

1 cup shortening

2 eggs, beaten

2 cups sifted flour

3 cups Quick Quaker Oats

11/ tsps. soda

1 tsp. salt

2 tsps. vanilla

Cream together the sugar and shortening.
Add beaten eggs and vanilla. Sift flour with
soda and salt, stir in Quaker Oats, and stir the
dry ingredients into the batter. Beat well. Put
14 cup sugar into a small bowl. Dip fingers
into the sugar, then pinch off a bit of dough
and roll it to the size and shape of a walnut.
Dip the ball into the sugar. Place balls on a
greased cookie sheet about three inches apart.
Bake at 375° for 12-15 minutes. This recipe
makes four dozen large soft cookies, or it may
be rolled, chilled, and sliced to make six dozen
thinner crisp cookies.
