Makes 5 dozen cookies.
1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter or
margarine
1/, cup sugar -
1, teaspoon salt

.~ 1 teaspoon vanilla or almond

extract, or 1/, teaspoon grated
lemon rind '

2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
(optional) ;

1 egg :

21, cups sifted all-purpose flour,

approximately

1. Let butter or margarine soften slighty
at room temperature; then cream with
sugar and salt until mixture is light and
fluffy. Add flavoring, cinnamon and egg
and blend thoroughly. Stir in enough
flour to make a soft workable dough.
Pack dough into cooky press or pastry
bag, using any pattern your prefer.

2. Lightly butter baking sheets. Press out
dough into rings, strips or any shape
you choose.

3. Bake in moderate oven (375°) for
about 10 minutes, or until cooky edges
are light golden brown.
