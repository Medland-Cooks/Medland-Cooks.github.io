Loaf Cake
3/4 cup butter
3/4 cup sugar
3 eggs, beaten
1/2 teaspoon vanilla
I cup cornstarch
1 cup all-purpose flour sifted with
1 teaspoon double-acting
baking powder

3 to 4 tablespoons milk to mix

Cream butter and sugar until
soft and light, add beaten eggs, and
vanilla. Fold in cornstarch sifted
with flour and baking powder. Add
milk to make a medium-soft
consistency. Spread in greased and
lined 2-pound loaf pan. Bake at 375
degrees on a rack placed in the
middle of the oven for 1-1/4 hours.
Cut in thin slices and spread with
butter if desired. Makes 8 to 10
Servings.
