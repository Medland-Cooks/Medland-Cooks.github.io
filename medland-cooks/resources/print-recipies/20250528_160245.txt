DIXIE PEANUT BRITTLE
2 c. granulated sugar
1 c. light corn syrup
Va2 c. water
12 tsp. salt
2 Thbsp. butter |
4 c. raw peanuts, skins on
2 teaspoons baking soda
In a heavy saucepan heat
sugar, syrup, water and salt to a
rolling boil. Add peanuts. Re-
duce heat to medium and stir
constantly. Cook until syrup
spins a thread (293° F.). Add
butter, then baking soda. Beat
rapidly and pour on a buttered
surface spreading to Y-inch
thickness. When cool break into
pieces. |
