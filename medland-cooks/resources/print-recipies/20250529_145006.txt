RECIPE NO. 807
SECRET KISS COOKIES Wd
FOODS
“a cookie for special occasions”
1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened 1 cup finely chopped nuts ~
Y2 cup granulated sugar 1 (9 oz.) pkg. chocolate kiss candies
1 tsp. vanilla Confectioner’s sugar
2 cups sifted flour
Beat butter, sugar, and vanilla at medium speed until light and fluffy. Add flour
and nuts and beat at low speed until blended. Chill dough. Remove foil from
kisses. Shape about 1 tablespoon of dough around each kiss and roll to make a
ball. Preheat oven to 375°F. and place cookies on ungreased cookie sheet.
Bake for 12 minutes, or until cookies are set but not brown. Cool slightly and roll
in confectioner’s sugar. Makes 3 to 4 dozen. Store in tightly covered container.
