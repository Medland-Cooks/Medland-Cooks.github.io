LENTIL SOUPv

14 c. dry lentils

15 small onion, finely chopped
(about 14 c.)

1 large rib celery with leaves,
finely chopped

1 small carrot, thinly sliced

2 ¢c. water

1 tsp. salt

Pinch of pepper

Wash and drain lentils. Put in a

saucepan with the onion, celery, car-

rots, water, salt and pepper. Heat to

boiling. Cover and simmer about 30

minutes or until lentils are tender.

Makes two servings of one cup each.
