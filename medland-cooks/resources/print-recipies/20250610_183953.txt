FUDGE FANCIES

1 cup light brown sugar

1 cup white sugar

14 cup shortening

1 5-0z. pkg. chocolate bits,

melted
3 eggs, beaten
2 cups sifted flour
| 1 tsp. soda

1 tsp. salt

1 tbsp. vanilla

1 cup chopped walnuts

Cream together the sugars and shortening.
Add cooled melted chocolate bits and eggs. Beat
well. Sift together flour, soda, and salt. Stir in
and beat until well blended. Add vanilla and
walnut meats. Shape into small balls. Place on
greased cookie sheet about three inches apart.
Bake at 375° for about 12 minutes. Makes about
five dozen small cookies.
