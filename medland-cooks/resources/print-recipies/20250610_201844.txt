NUTS AND BOLTS L 4 ’/ 1,
“A tasty mixture for snacking” WO DS
2 cups Chex or Kix Cereal 2 cup (1 stick) butter or
1 cup Cheerios margarine
2 cups tiny cheese crackers 1 tsp. Worchestershire sauce
2 cups pretzel sticks Ya tsp. garlic salt
Y2 Ib. mixed nuts Y4 tsp. celery salt
Combine the cereal, crackers, pretzels and nuts in shallow pan (9x13-in.).
Melt butter, add seasonings and add to the mixture in pan. Stir and salt
lightly. Bake slowly in 250° oven for 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes. Cool
and serve as a snack. Store in tightly covered container.
