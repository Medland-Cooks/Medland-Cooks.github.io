OLD SPICE

2 cups brown sugar

1 cup shortening

2 eggs, beaten

14 cup sour milk

2 tsps. cinnamon

1 tsp. cloves

1 tsp. nutmeg

3 cups sifted flour

1 tsp. soda

1 tsp. salt

14 cup chopped walnuts

15 cup seedless raisins

Cream together the sugar and shortening.
Add the beaten eggs and milk and beat well.
Sift together the flour, soda, and salt, add the
spices, and stir into the batter. Beat well. Add
the nuts and raisins and mix in until blended.
Drop about a tablespoonful of dough for each
cookie on greased baking sheet. Keep the cookies
about three inches apart. Bake at 375° for 12-15
minutes. |
