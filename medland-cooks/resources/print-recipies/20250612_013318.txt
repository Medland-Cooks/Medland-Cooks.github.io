Melt butter or margarine in medium saucepan;
remove from heat. Add next three ingredients and
stir until sugar is dissolved. Sift flour and soda to-
gether; stir into orange juice mixture. Add eggand
blend well. Fold in dates and pecans. Spread in
greased 9 x 9 x 2-inch baking pan. Bake in a pre-
heated moderate oven (350°F.) to 25 minutes, or
until done. Cool completely in pan. Combine re-
maining two ingredients for glaze. Drizzle over
surface. When set, cut into bars. Makes 24 (about
3 x 1-inch) bars.
