ANGEL FLUFF FROSTING '
Sift 1 Ib. (4 to 5 cups) powdered sugar. Add about a third of it to 4 tablespoons
soft or melted butter or margarine. Mix thoroughly. Beat in 2 egg whites, one at
a time, beating hard after addition of each. Gradually add rest of the sugar with
| or 2 tablespoons cream. Tint with Schilling food color. If various colors are
desired, divide frosting into smaller bowls and fint each bowl of frosting to desired
color. This frosting works beautifully in a decorating set or may be swirled on
with a knife. To write names, thin frosting slightly. Cover with a damp cloth when
not in use to prevent crusting over.
