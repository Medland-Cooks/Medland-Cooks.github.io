CRANBERRY COOLER
34 cup sieved cranberry sauce 1 pint lemon ice
14 cup lemon juice 1 large bottle carbonated water
Mash cranberry sauce and lemon juice together in a bowl. Place 3 table-
spoons of mixture in tall glass, fill 34 full with carbonated water, and add
lemon ice to fill.
PINEAPPLE SODA
8 tablespoons crushed pineapple 16 cup sparkling water
1 scoop vanilla ice cream
Place 3 tablespoons crushed pineapple and juice in bottom of tall glass.
Add 15 cup sparkling water and scoop of vanilla ice cream. Stir, then fill
with sparkling water and serve. (Strawberry, peach, or orange marmalade
may be used in place of pineapple.)
CHOCOLATE ICE CREAM SODA
2 tablespoons chocolate syrup Carbonated water
| scoop ice cream
Place 2 tablespoons chocolate syrup in bottom of glass, then add 14 cup
carbonated water and scoop of ice cream. Blend, fill with carbonated water,
and serve.
LEMONADE BASE TO STORE IN REFRIGERATOR
2 cups sugar Juice of 6 lemons
2 cups water Grated peel of 2 lemons
1 cup mint leaves Lemon slices
Juice and grated peel of 2 oranges Sprig of mint
Add 2 cups sugar to 2 cups water, and cook 5 minutes. Pour over 1 cup
mint leaves in a bowl. Add juice and grated peel of 2 oranges, juice of
6 lemons, and grated peel of 2 lemons. Cover and steep for 1 hour. Strain,
place in glass container, cover, and store in refrigerator. Using 14 cup ol
this base, add water and ice cubes for serving. Garnish with lemon slice and
sprig of mint.
BOTTLED DRINK SODAS
Combine any of the following with a scoop of ice cream: ginger ale, root
beer, any of the colas, grape juice combined with ginger ale. Blend and
serve.
