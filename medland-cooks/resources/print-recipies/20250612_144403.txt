1 cup plus 2 tahlespoons red or yellow food color

1. Start heating oven to 375°F, Grease, then line with wax paper
2 S-inch layer-cake pans. Sift flour, baking powder and salt to-
gether 3 times.

2. In large bowl, with an electric mixer at high speed, beat egg
whites until soft mounds form. Gradually beat in 1 cup sugar;
continue to beat until soft, but firm, peaks form; set aside.

3. In medium bowl, with mixer at medium speed, cream together 1
Cup sugar and margarine until very light, fluffy and cream colored.

4. Next, at low speed, beat in, until just smooth, a quarter of the
flour mixture, then a third of the milk; repeat 2 times; then
beat in remaining flour and vanilla and almond extracts. (As
milk is added each time, mixture may appear curdled.) Pour bat-
ter over egg-white mixture and fold two together until smooth.

5. Turn into pans; bake 25 to 30 minutes, or until a cake tester,
inserted in center, comes out clean. Cool on cake rack for 10
to 15 minutes, then turn out of pan, peel off wax paper and let
finish cooling.

6. When cold, fill and frost with frosting. For a pleasant touch,
stir in a few drops red or yellow food color to give frosting
a delicate-pink or golden-yellow cast. Makes 16 servings. e
