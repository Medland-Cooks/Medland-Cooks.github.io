JOHNNY CARSON’s salad reminds us of his TV
style—crisp, colorful, with a slight bite (here
it's horse-radish!).

CHRISTMAS WALDORF
Make day hefore, or several hours hefore serv-
ing:

Snip V2 cup pitted dates, coarsely; chop 2
cup canned California walnuts, coarsely; chop
1 large stalk celery; wrap each, separately. Re-
frigerate, along with 1 cup fresh or thawed fro-
zen cranberries, a washed and dried lettuce
head and 3% to 1 cup mayonnaise, mixed with
2 tablespoons horse-radish.

About twenty minutes before serving:

In bowl toss dates with walnuts, celery, cran-
berries and 1 medium red apple, cored, cubed.
Now lightly toss with mayonnaise mixture. Line
white salad bowl with lettuce cups; pile apple
mixture in center, with a few mint sprigs
garnishing edges of lettuce leaves. Makes 4
or 5 servings.
