Mincemeat Pecan Pie

1 unbaked 9-inch pastry shell
1 (9-ounce) package dry condensed

mincemeat
Y4 cup margarine
2/3 cup light or dark corn syrup
% cup sugar
% cup sherry
2 eggs, well beaten
7% cup coarsely chopped pecans

Break block of mincemeat into
small pieces. Melt margarine in
saucepan. Remove from heat. Add
corn syrup, sugar, sherry and
mincemeat to saucepan. Cook
stirring constantly, until mixture
comes to boil. Gradually stir into
beaten eggs. Mix in nuts. Pour into
pastry shell. Bake in 375 degrees F.
oven until top springs back when
lightly pressed with finger, about
50 minutes. Cool before serving.
