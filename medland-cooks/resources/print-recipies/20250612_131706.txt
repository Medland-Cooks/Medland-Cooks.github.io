CRUNCHY WALNUT BREAD

Make day before:

1. Start heating oven to 350°F. In bowl, with
fork, beat 1 egg with 3 cup milk and %
cup granulated sugar until blended.

2. Stir in 3 cups packaged biscuit mix until
just moistened, then 1 cup chopped Cali-
fornia walnuts. |

3. Pour into well-greased 9” by 5” by 3” loaf
pan. Bake 1 hour or until cake tester, in-
serted in center, comes out clean. Cool com-
pletely, then store, wrapped in foil, until
sliced next day. Makes 1 loaf.

Party Bread: Grease well 2 empty 8-ounce cans

(used for walnuts). Make walnut-bread batter;

pour into cans. Bake at 350°F. 45 minutes or

till done.

Orange Marmalade Nut Bread: Make and bake

as above, adding Y4 cup orange marmalade in

step 2.
