BERRY SWITZER SPRITZER
1 generous pint very ripe
strawberries, with a few of best-
looking ones saved for garnish

34 c. sugar

2 ¢c. water

Sparkling water

Ice

Wash and hull the strawberries.

Bring sugar and 2 cups water to a

boil, stirring to make sure all the

sugar granules dissolve before the

boiling point is reached. When the

- syrup is bubbling hard, add the

strawberries and immediately re-

move the pan from the stove. Cover

with lid and leave berries to soak

until cold. Blend or process, then put

through a fine sieve to remove seeds.
