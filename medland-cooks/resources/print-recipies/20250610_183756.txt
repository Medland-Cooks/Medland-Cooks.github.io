MUSTER DAY GINGERBREAD
1 cup shortening
1 cup hot water
1 cup dark molasses
1 cup sugar
1 cup flour
1 tsp. soda
1 tsp. salt
1 tsp. ginger
1 tsp. nutmeg (optional)
2 tsps. cinnamon

In mixing bowl melt shortening in hot water.
Stir in molasses and sugar. Sift together flour,
Soda) salt, ginger, nutmeg, and cinnamon. Mix
them, a little at a time, into the batter. Then
add enough additional sifted flour (about b5
cups) to make a stiff dough. Roll the dough on
a floured board 14 inch thick. Pat it into a
greased cookie sheet or pan (8 x 16 or 10 x 15
inches). Bake 20 minutes at 375°.

This gingerbread is made from an old
receipt. It was first served at a muster in Deer-
field, Massachusetts, in 1816. The receipt has
been adapted for present-day use by the Village
Bake-House.
