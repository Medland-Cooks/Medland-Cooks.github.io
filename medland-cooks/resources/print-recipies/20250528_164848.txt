Mix flour, cinnamon, ginger, soda
and salt together and stir into the
molasses mixture until dough is-
smooth and satiny. (At this point,
dough is extremely soft, but it firms
up when it is refrigerated.)
Divide into 2 portions. Wrap in plas-
tic wrap and chill for at least 2
hours. (Can be refrigerated for sev-
eral days if necessary.) Let stand at
room temperature a few minutes un-
til it is pliable enough to roll.
Roll out and cut with your favorite
cookie cutters. No flour is needed to
roll out these cookies. Bake on light-
ly greased tins at 375 degrees for 7 to
10 minutes. Baking time depends on
~ size and thickness of cookie.
