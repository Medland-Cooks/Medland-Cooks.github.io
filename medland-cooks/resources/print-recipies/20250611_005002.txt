CHESTNUT STUFFING
4 cups (1 bunch) chopped celery
1 cup chopped onion
% cup fresh minced parsley
5 cup butter
8 cups dry white bread cubes
13 cups chopped, cooked chestnuts
2 tsSp. salt
2 tsp. basil
2 tsp. sage
% cup half & half
2 TBSP white wine (optional)
Cook celery, onion, and parsley in butter
until tender but not browned. Add to bread
cubes along with chestnuts, seasonings,
cream, and wine (if desired). Toss lightly
toimix. ‘Makes 1l cups of stuffing, or
enough for a 13 to 16 pound turkey.
