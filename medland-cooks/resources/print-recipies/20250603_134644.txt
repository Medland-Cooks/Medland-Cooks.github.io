TROPICAL BANANA COOKIES
(Makes about 4 dozen 214 jnch cookies)
1 package Duncan Hined 34 cup mashed ripe bananas
Banana Supreme Deluxe Cake Mix (2 medium bananas)
14 cup cooking oil pecan halves, if desired
1 egg
Preheat oven to 350°.
Combine all ingredients and mix well. Drop from a teaspoon
onto an ungreased cookie sheet. If desired, top each cookie
with a pecan half.
Bake at 350° for 10-12 minutes, untij| golden.
Cool on cookie sheet about 1 minute, then remove to rack to
finish cooling.
