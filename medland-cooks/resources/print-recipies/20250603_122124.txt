GLOSSY BROWNIES
To Be Presented on "Take 10 With Virginia" Sept. 13, 1972
From the Kitchen of Florygene Bonebright
1 120z. Package Chocolate Chips
(melt in double boiler)
2/3 Cup Shortening
4 Eggs
1 Cup Sugar
Cream eggs and sugar for 10 minutes. Add T cup
flour, 1 teaspoon baking powder, 1/2 teaspoon salt
and 2 teaspoons vanilla to the cream mixture, mix
well.
Add melted mixture and T cup nuts.
Bake in 9 x 12 pan. 375 degrees for 25 minutes.
