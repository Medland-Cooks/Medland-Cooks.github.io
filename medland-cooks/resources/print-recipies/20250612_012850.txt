HORS D’OEUVRE DATES

12 DROMEDARY Pitted Dates

12 pecan or walnut halves

2 cup clear French or Italian dressing

6 bacon slices, halved

Fill each date with a nut. Marinate several hours
in dressing. Wrap in half strip of bacon; secure
with a wooden pick. Broil quickly on each side,
until bacon is brown and crisp. Serve immediately.
Makes 12 dates.
