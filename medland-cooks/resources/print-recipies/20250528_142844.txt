Refreshing . . . Tangy . . . Colorful
2 cups cranberry sauce 1 cup mashed ripe bananas*
3 tablespoons sugar (2 to 3 bananas) ;
3 tablespoons orange juice 1 cup heavy cream, whipped
*Use fully ripe bananas . . . yellow peel flecked with brown
Mash cranberry sauce with a fork. Stir sugar into orange juice,
mixing until dissolved. Add orange juice and banana to qranber.ry
sauce and mix well. Turn into freezing tray of automatic refrig-
erator and freeze, with indicator at coldest setting, until mixture 1s
nartially set. Turn mixture into a chilled bowl. Beat with rotary
egg beater until smooth. Fold in whipped cream. Return to tray
and freeze until firm.
Makes 4 to 6 servings.
. Important: One l-pound can (2-cups) jellied cranberry sauce or
sauce made from fresh cranberries may be used.
Delicious and Nourishing . . .
With or Between Meals
1 fully ripe banana* 1 cup (8 ounces) COLD milk
*Use fully ripe banana . . . peel well flecked with brown
Peel banana. Slice into a bowl and beat with a rotary egg beater
or electric mixer until smooth and creamy. Add milk and mix
thoroughly. Serve immediately.
Makes 1 large drink or 2 medium-sized drinks.
