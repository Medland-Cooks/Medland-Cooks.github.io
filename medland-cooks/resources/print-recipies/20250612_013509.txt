DATE-NUT CAKE
34 cup boiling water
1 cup DROMEDARY Pitted Dates, snipped or
DROMEDARY Chopped Dates
/3 cup butter or margarine
12 cup granulated sugar
1egg
1 teaspoon grated lemon rind
112 cups sifted all-purpose flour
1 teaspoon baking soda
34 teaspoon salt
12 cup chopped walnuts
Confectioners’ sugar
Pour water over dates; cool. Beat butter or mar-
garine and sugar until creamy; add egg and beat
until fluffy. Stir in lemon rind. Sift together next
three ingredients; stir alternately with date mix-
ture into butter mixture. Stir in nuts. Spread in a
greased 8x8x2-inch baking pan. Bake in a pre-
heated moderate oven (350°F.) 25 to 30 minutes,
or until done. Cool on rack. Sprinkle with con-
- fectioners’ sugar. Makes 16 (about 2-inch) squares.
