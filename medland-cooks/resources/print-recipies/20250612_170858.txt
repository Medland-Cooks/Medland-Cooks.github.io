CRISPY CARAMEL POPCORN

1 cup sugar .

Y2 cup butter or margarine

Y2 cup dark corn syrup :

Y2 teaspoon salt, or to taste

%2 teaspoon vanilla extract

4 quarts popped corn
1. Stir together sugar, butter, corn syrup, salt
and vanilla in 1%-quart saucepan.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constant-
ly, until mixture comes to a boil; boil 5
minutes. Remove from heat; pour syrup mix-
ture over popped corn in large, shallow bak-
ing pan. Stir to coat well.
3. Bake at 250 F for 1 hour, stirring every
15 minutes. _ |
4. Remove from oven and stir mixture oc-
casionally to prevent it from sticking together
while cooling. Store in tightly covered
container. Makes 1 Ib.
