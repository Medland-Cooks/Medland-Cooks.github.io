Irish Stew

2 pounds lamb or mutton stew meat
4 cups water
3 carrots, sliced into 14 inch slices
1 small turnip, diced
1 onion, sliced
2 teaspoons salt
/4 teaspoon pepper
1 Dbay leaf
3 tablespoons chopped parsley
2 tablespoons flour

Cut the meat into 2-inch pieces.
Place in a kettle and add the water.
Simmer 2 hours. Then add the re-
maining ingredients except flour and
cook until vegetables are tender, about
15 hour. Thicken the broth with the
flour mixed with % cup cold water.
NOTE: Dumplings may be cooked on
top of the thickened stew. Yield: 6
servings. Cooking time: 2% hours.
