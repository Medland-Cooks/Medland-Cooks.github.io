SWEET ‘N SPICY HAM

2 cups pineapple juice

1% cup firmly packed light brown
sugar A

1 teaspoon cinnamon

1, teaspoon ginger

4 teaspoon cloves

4 to 5 pound boneless fully-cooked
ham haif

Combine pineapple juice, sugar,
cinnamon, ginger and cloves in small
heavy saucepan; simmer 6 to 8
minutes.

Score fat on top of ham surface in
scallop pattern. Place ham on rack in
shallow roasting pan. Insert meat
thermometer, placing tip in center of
thickest part of meat. Bake at 350
degrees F. about 1 to 12 hours or
until internal temperature reaches
130 degrees K. to 140 degrees F.,
basting ham every 15 to 20 minutes
during baking with pineapple glaze.
To serve, spoon remaining warm

- glaze over ham slices. Makes 8 to 10
servings. ‘
