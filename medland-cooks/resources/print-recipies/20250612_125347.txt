Make day before serving as follows:

1. In bowl, with spoon, thoroughly blend cream
cheese with Y2 cup butter or margarine; then
stir in flour. Refrigerate dough about 1 hour.

2. Then start heating oven to 325°F, Also shape
dough into 24 134-inch balls, then place
one in each greased cup of muffin pans.
Press dough to bottom and sides of each cup,
as a smooth lining.

3. In small bowl, with electric mixer at medium
speed, beat together 1 tablespoon butter or
margarine, egg, brown sugar, vanilla and salt
until smooth.

4. Divide pecans in half. Use half to sprinkle on
bottom of pastry-lined muffin-pan cups; fill
each with about 1 teaspoon egg mixture; top
with rest of pecans. Bake 25 minutes; cool,
then carefully remove from pans. Makes 24.
