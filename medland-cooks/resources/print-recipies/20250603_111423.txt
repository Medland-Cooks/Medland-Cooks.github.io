SWEET DILLS

Make syrup of:

4 cups sugar

1 quart vinegar

1 pint water |

% cup salt

% teaspoon celery seed

Wash and put pickles in jars. Put a

sprig of dill and two slices of onion

in bottom of each jar. On top of
" pickles pour boiling syrup over pick-

les. These are ready to use in four

weeks.
