GOLDEN POPCORN BALLS

Several days before serving:

1. In saucepan mix together 1 cup granulated
sugar, %2 cup white corn syrup and 2 table-
spoons soft butter or margarine. Stir, over
low heat, until mixture comes to a boil.
Cook, covered, about 2 minutes.

2. Remove cover, place candy thermometer in
mixture, then cook, stirring occasionally, un-
til thermometer reads 240°F., or until a bit
of syrup, dropped into cold water, forms a
soft ball. Meanwhile place 8 or 9 cups
popped corn (3%2-ounce bag) in large kettle.

3. When done, remove syrup from heat, and
stir in 1 teaspoon vanilla extract and Y
teaspoon salt. With long spoon, stir pop-
corn, while pouring syrup over it, mixing
rapidly and being sure all kernels are
coated.

4. Wet hands in cold water, then quickly press
some of mixture—about a cupful—into a
ball shape, Place on wax-paper-lined tray to
cool. Repeat with rest of mixture. Store,
saran-wrapped, at room temperature. Makes
7 or 8 balls.
