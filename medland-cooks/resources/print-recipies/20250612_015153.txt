PINEAPPLE-STRAWBERRY
JAM WITH RUM

- 12-ounce box of frozen straw-
berries, thawed

1 No. 2 can crushed pineapple

5 cups sugar

1 teaspoon grated lemon rind

Y teaspoon cinnamon

12 bottle liquid fruit pectin (such as
Certo)

3 tablespoons dark rum

Combine strawberries, pineapple,
sugar and lemon rind in a very large
saucepan. Bring to a boil and boil
hard for one minute. Remove from
heat and stir in light rum and pectin.
Stir and skim for four minutes. Ladle
into hot sterilized jars to % inch from |
the top. Cover with hot sterilized lids
and screw on tight. Invert until cool,
then turn upright. Yield is eight to ‘
nine half pints. .
