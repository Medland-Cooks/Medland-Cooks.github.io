‘ CHRISTMAS PUNCH

About twenty minutes before serving:

In large saucepan combine 2 quarts apple
cider, 2 quarts ginger ale and 2 cinnamon
sticks; simmer about 5 minutes. Serve hot,
from bowl or pitcher, tied with red ribbon and
holly, into punch cups or mugs. (For adults you
may add 1 pint vodka, just before serving, if
desired.) Makes 16 to 20 servings. &
