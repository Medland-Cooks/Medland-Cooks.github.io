Noodle Hot Dish
1 pound wide noodles
2 pounds boneless veal, cut up
115 pounds pork, cut up
Boil few soup bones for broth or use
bouillon cubes
4 to 6 large onions, chopped
4 to 6 green peppers, chopped
Brown meat, onion and peppers.
Add salt and pepper to taste. Cook
about an hour; taste and add more
water if necessary. One half package
of onion soup may be added for extra
flavor. After meat is cooked and
noodles have been cooked and drained
alternate layers in flat. pan or small
roaster. If there is not enough broth
add tomato juice, just enough to cover
and bake slowly for one hour. Top
with grated cheese one-half hour be-
fore serving. Serves 20.
