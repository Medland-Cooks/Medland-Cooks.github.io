PENNSYLVANIA DUTCH
These cookies used to be given to the
masked revelers (Belsnickles) who
went bell-ringing from one house to
another on Christmas Eve.
Bake at 400° for 8 to 10 minutes.
Makes 4 to 5 dozen cookies.

3. Bake in hot oven (400°) for 8 to 10

minutes, or until faintly golden edged.
