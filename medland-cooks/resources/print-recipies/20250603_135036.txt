1 Recipe Sweet Roll Dough
12 cup melted butter or margarine
1 cup brown sugar
112 teaspoons Schilling Pumpkin Pie Spice
15 cup finely chopped nuts . 15 cup raisins
Prepare dough using your favorite recipe or hot roll mix. Let rise then
shape into small balls about 1 inch in diameter. Roll each ball in the melted
butter, then in the brown sugar, Pumpkin Pie Spice and nuts which have
been thoroughly mixed. Place a layer of balls so they barely touch in a
well-greased 9-inch tube pan. Sprinkle with raisins. Add another layer and
sprinkle with more raisins. Continue in this manner until all the dough is
used. Let rise 45 minutes. Bake in a 375° oven about 1 hour. Loosen from
pan and remove. If it begins to break, tie sides with string. 1 :
