DEAR HELOISE: At one time in
your column, you had a tip on what
to put in devil’s food cake mix to
make brownies. We really enjoyed
them but I have lost your instruc-
tions. Could you please reprint
them? — FRANCES BENOIT

These brownies are hard to resist
and so easy to make. I find it’s bet-
ter to use a devil’'s food cake mix
that has a pudding mix added as it’s
richer — which of course makes for
better brownies.

To this mix, add one egg and one-
fourth cup water. One-half cup
choppped pecans also may be add-
ed, if desired.

Different brands of mix may call
for varying amounts of water but
start with the one-fourth cup, add-
ing more if needed, one teaspoonful
at a time. The batter should be very
thick.

After mixing thoroughly, spoon
the batter into a greased pan,
smoothing it out evenly. Bake at
350F, for 20 to 25 minutes for a
chewy brownie, a little longer for a

~ drier, cake type.
