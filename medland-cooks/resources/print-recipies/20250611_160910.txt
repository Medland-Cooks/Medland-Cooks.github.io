STUFFED CHICKEN BREASTS

SUPREME*

Preparation time: 25 minutes.

Bake at 400° for 40 minutes.

Makes 8 servings at 44¢ each.

4 whole chicken breasts (about
12 ounces each)

1 package (6 ounces) process
Gruyere cheese, shredded

4 pound salami, chopped

1, cup chopped green onions

1 egg

1 package (2% ounces) seasoned
coating mix for chicken

14 cup (Y2 stick) butter or margarine

Y4 cup flour ,

2 cups milk -

1. Halve chicken breasts; remove skin, if
you wish, then cut meat in one piece
from bones. Place each chicken breast
between two sheets of wax paper and
pound with a wooden mallet to thin.

2. Combine 1 cup of the cheese, salami
and green omions in small bowl. Place
about % cup of cheese filling in the
center of each chicken breast. Roll up
tightly and fasten with a pick.

3. Beat egg in a shallow dish; place sea-
soned coating mix on wax paper. Dip
stuffed breasts in egg; roll in seasoned
coating mix. Place in single layer in
greased large baking dish.

4. Bake in hot oven (400°) 40 minutes, or
until golden-brown.

5. While chicken is baking, make cheese
sauce. Melt butter or margarine in a

- medium-size saucepan; stir in flour;
cook, stirring constantly, just until
bubbly. Stir in milk; continue cooking
and stirring until sauce thickens and
bubbles 1 minute; stir in remaining
cheese until melted. Serve with chicken.
