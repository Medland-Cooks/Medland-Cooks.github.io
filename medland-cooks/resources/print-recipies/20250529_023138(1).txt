Ham-Chicken Bake
Call on this recipe when you want an easy company meal—
1 pound cooked or 3 tablespoons enriched %2 cup chopped onion
canned ham, sliced flour 1 3-ounce can (%4 cup)
2 cups sliced cooked 24 cup chicken broth broiled sliced
or canned chicken 24 cup light cream mushrooms, drained
3 tablespoons butter Dash salt b el o
or margarine Dash pepper Grated Parmesan cheese
Alternate layers of ham and chicken in 10x6x1V4-inch baking dish. Melt butter;
blend in flour. Stir in broth and cream gradually; cook, stirring constantly, till thick.
Add remaining ingredients except cheese. Pour sauce over ham and chicken and
sprinkle with cheese. Bake in moderate oven (350°) 30 to 40 minutes. Makes 6 to 8
servings.
Mrs. Francis D. Lewis, Brackney, Pennsylvania
