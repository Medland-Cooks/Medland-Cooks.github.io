DANNY THOMAS invites you to taste a Lebanese
dip called Homos Be Tahinee. Our translation:
simply marvelous!

HOMOS BE TAHINEE (Lebanese Appetizer)

1 16-ounce can garhanzos (chick peas)

3 tablespoons Tahinee*

3 tablespoons water
6 tablespoons lemon juice
1 to 4 cloves garlic, minced
2 teaspoon salt
1 teaspoon dried parsley flakes
parsley sprigs
pitted ripe olives
celery hearts
scallions
Lebanese, French, Italian or rye bread
Several days before serving:

1. Drain garbanzos; then shell off thin skins.
Place them in large bowl and mash coarsely.
2. In another bowl| place Tahinee, water, lemon
juice, garlic, salt, parsley flakes; mix well.
3. Pour this garlic mixture into mashed gar-
banzos and blend well. Cover, refrigerate.
About ten minutes before serving:

Line flat serving dish with parsley sSprigs.
Turn Homos Be Tahinee into a serving bowl;
place in center of parsley-lined dish, surround-
ed with olives, celery hearts, scallions and
chunks of bread. Makes about 2 cups dip for
dunking.

*You may order Tahinee (a sesame-seed prod-
uct) from Trinacria Importing Co., 415 Third
Avenue, New York, New York.
