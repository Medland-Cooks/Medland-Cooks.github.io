FRUITED TUNA SALAD

2 (7-ounce) cans tuna, drained

1 cup DROMEDARY Pitted Dates, quartered

1 cup sliced celery

1 medium apple, cored and diced

1/2 cup mayonnaise

2 tablespoons minced onion

2 teaspoons lemon juice

1 teaspoon curry powder

Lettuce cups a
Combine first four ingredients in medium mixing
bowl. Blend together next four ingredients; pour
over tuna mixture and toss gently. Chill. Spoon
into lettuce cups. Makes 6 (about 34 cup) servings.
