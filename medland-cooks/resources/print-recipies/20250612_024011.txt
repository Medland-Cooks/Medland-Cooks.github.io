CHEF D’OEUVRE

18 shucked oysters 9 bacon strips, halved

lemon juice 18 buttered Melba-toast rounds

cayenne pepper parsley sprays

About thirty minutes before serving as appetizer:

1. Preheat broiler 10 minutes or as manufacturer directs. Sprinkle
drained oysters with lemon juice; lightly with cayenne pepper.
Wrap each oyster in a half bacon strip, secure with toothpick.

2. Broil oysters, about 4 inches below heat, turning until bacon is
well browned. Remove picks, then place each oyster on a Melba-
toast round; garnish with parsley. Makes 18, so plan to allow 3
oyster appetizers apiece.
