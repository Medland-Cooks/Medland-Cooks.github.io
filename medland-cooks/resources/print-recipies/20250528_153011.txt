Cherry n’ ice cream pie Yield: 1 (9-inch) pie

1 (1 pound 1 ounce) dark, 1/4 teaspoon almond extract
pitted cherries in heavy syrup, 1-2 drops red food coloring

2/3 cup granulated sugar 1 baked 9-inch pastry shell, cooled
Chill cream, beaters, and small mixing bowl. Meanwhile, cut cherries in half and
set aside. Pour cream into chilled bow!. Beat on Speed 9. Gradually add sugar.
Continue beating until soft peaks form, scraping sides of bowl as necessary. Add
vanilla extract, almond extract, and food coloring. DO NOT OVE'RBEA.T! _Stop mixer
and fold in cherries. Spoon into prepared pastry shell, alternating with ice cream.
Freeze for at least 2 hours before serving.

Key lime pie Yield: 1 (9-inch) pie
1 package (3 ounce) lime 1 1/3 cups (15 ounce) sweetened
gelatin condensed milk
3/4 cup boiling water 1/4 teaspoon aromatic bitters
1 tablespoon grated lime rind 2 drops green food coloring
Juice of two limes or 1/2 cup 2 egg whites
lime juice 1 baked 9-inch pastry shell,
2 8gg yolks cooled
Dissolve gelatin in boiling water. Add lime rind and juice to the gelatin. In large
mixing bow!, beat egg yolks for a few seconds on Speed 2. Slowly add gelatin,
condensed milk, bitters and food coloring and continue mixing on Speed 2 until
thoroughly blended. Chill mixture until slightly thickened. In small bowl! beat egg
whites on Speed 12 until stiff peaks form. Fold egg whites into gelatin mixture on
Speed 1. Pour into pastry shell. Chill until firm. Garnish with prepared whipped
topping and lime slices or shaved chocolate, if desired.
