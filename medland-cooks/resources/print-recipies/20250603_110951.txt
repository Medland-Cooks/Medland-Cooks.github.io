BEER BATTER FOR
WALL-EYES
L4 c. cornstarch
Iz c. flour
1z c. beer
2 egg whites, whipped
until very stiff
6 walleye fillets
Sift together the corn-
starch and flour, Add the
-beer and mix until the bat-
ter is smooth. Fold in the
egg whites. Season the
fish fillets well and dip in
the batter. Fry in about
one-inch of hot fat until
the fillets are well-
browned on both sides and
the fish is done,
