HARVARD BEETS

Early on the day of serving:

1. Cut tops from 2 pounds fresh beets, leaving
1 inch of stem, also root end. Cook, cov-
ered, in boiling salted water to cover, 30 to
45 minutes or until fork-tender.

2. Drain, peel, then slice beets thinly—there
should be about 3 cups. Cover and refrig-
erate.

About thirty minutes before serving:

In double boiler, blend %3 to V2 cup gran-
ulated sugar, 1 tablespoon cornstarch, 12 cup
white vinegar and Y4 teaspoon salt; cook, over
boiling water, stirring constantly, until smooth
and slightly thickened. Add Y4 cup butter or
margarine, 1 tablespoon orange marmalade,
and beets; heat about 20 minutes; then keep
covered until served. Makes about 6 servings.
