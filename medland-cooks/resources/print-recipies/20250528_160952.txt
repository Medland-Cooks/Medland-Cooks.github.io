MADIGAN'’S VELVET TROUSERS
1 pkg. (% oz.) unflavored gelatin
Ya c. cold water
1 ¢. whipping cream
1'% tbsp. clear honey
3 tbsp. Irish whiskey (Jameson)
Soak gelatin in cold water in small metal
measuring cup; set measuring cup in
saucepan of gently boiling water; stir until
gelatin dissolves (about three minutes).
Remove from pan of water; set aside.
Pour cream into small well-chilled bowl and
whip until light and thick.
Slowly add honey and whiskey, mixing
well.
Place bowl in pan of ice water; fold in
dissolved gelatin. Continue to mix and
gently fold mixture until it begins to set.
Spoon into four small glass serving dishes
or parfait glasses; serve immediately or
refrigerate until ready to serve.
Garnish with fresh mint leaf or color the
mixture with a couple of drops of green
food coloring when mixing together.
Adapted from “Irish Cooking,"’ by Ruth Bauder
Kershner, Weathervane Books.
