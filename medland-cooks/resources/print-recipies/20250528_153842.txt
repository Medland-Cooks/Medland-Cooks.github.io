INDOOR S’MORES/

% c. light corn syrup

2 tbsp. margarine or butter

1 (1114-0z.) pkg. milk chocolate

chips

1 tsp. vanilla |

8 ¢. Golden Grahams cereal

3 ¢. miniature marshmallows

Butter a rectangular pan, 13 by 9 by

2 inches. Heat corn syrup, margarine

and chocolate chips to boiling in a 3-

quart saucepan, stirring constantly.

Remove from heat. Stir in vanilla.

Pour over cereal in a large mixing

bowl; toss quickly until completely

coated with chocolate. Fold in

marshmallows, 1 cup at a time.

Press mixture evenly in pan with

buttered back of spoon.

Let stand until firm, at least 1 hour.

Cut into about 1l4-inch squares.

Store loosely covered at room tem-
~ perature up to 2 days. Makes 48

squares. |
