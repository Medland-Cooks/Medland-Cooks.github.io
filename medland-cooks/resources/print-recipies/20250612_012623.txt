DATE APPETIZER PINWHEELS

1 cup DROMEDARY Pitted Dates, snipped or

DROMEDARY Chopped Dates
2 cup water
2 tablespoons cider vinegar
8 ounces sausage links or Italian sausage,

cooked, finely crumbled
/3 cup finely chopped walnuts
Ya cup chutney, finely chopped, optional
2 (8-ounce) packages refrigerated crescent

dinner rolls
4 ounces sharp Cheddar cheese, grated (1 cup)
Simmer dates in water and vinegar for 5 minutes
in covered saucepan. Remove from heat; mix in
next three ingredients; cool. Place refrigerated
crescent dinner rolls on floured board; pinch to-
gether seams. Roll into 2 (12 x 8-inch) rectangles.
Sprinkle with cheese. Spread with date filling. Roll
up tightly from long side. Wrap in wax paper and
chill. Cut into 3/s-inch slices and place on greased
baking sheets. Bake in a preheated hot oven
(425°F.) 8 to 10 minutes, or until browned. Serve g
warm. Makes 5 dozen (about 172-inch) pinwheels.
