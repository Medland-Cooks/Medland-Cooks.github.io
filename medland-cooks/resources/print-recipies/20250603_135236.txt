6 apples - 14 cup granulated sugar
3 tablespoons water
14 teaspoon Schilling Pumpkin Pie Spice
1 cup sifted all-purpose flour

15 cup brown sugar . 14 cup butter or margarine
Pare and clice apples and place in a greased 1%, quart casserole dish. Add
granulated sugar and water. Combine spice, flour and brown sugar. Cut in
butter or margarine with pastry blender until crumbly. Sprinkle over
apples. Bake uncovered in a 350°F. oven for 1 hour. Serves 6. :

Another fine recipe from the world-famed kitchens of McCormick-Schilling
