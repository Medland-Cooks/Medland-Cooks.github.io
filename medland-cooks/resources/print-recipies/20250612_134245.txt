STUFFED BRUSSELS SPROUTS ‘

Early day of serving: ‘

1. Thaw 2 10-ounce packages frozen Brussels
sprouts; then, with paring knife, cut a tiny
core, Va-inch to Y2-inch deep, from center
of each (save these to cook with soup or
other vegetables).

2. Meanwhile, in electric blender or small
bow!, with fork, blend 1 8-ounce package
cream cheese, 3 tablespoons finely-snipped

| chives, and 2 to 3 tablespoons milk, until
like whipped cream.

3. Pat sprouts dry with paper toweling, then
cut a small slice from rounded end, so each
sprout “sits up” straight. Now put cream-
cheese mixture in pastry bag with deco-
rating tube number 19 in place.

Use to fill sprouts as pictured; refrigerate.
At serving time:

Serve sprouts, with Beef Tartare (page 236)
on tray. Or serve as separate nibbler. Makes
about 30 servings.

P.S. Cleaned, raw, fresh Brussels sprouts may

replace frozen ones.
