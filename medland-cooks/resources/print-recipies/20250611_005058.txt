You might wish to make marzipan candies
a holiday tradition for your family. Here
are recipes for Homemade Almond Paste and
Marzipan:
Homemade Almond Paste:s
Grind 13 cups whole blanched almonds in a
food processor, or, a portion at a time
in an electric blender. Combine with 1%
cups sifted powdered sugar, 1 egg white,
1 tsp. almond extract, and + tsp. salt.
Work to a stiff paste. Makes 1 1/3 cups.
Will keep for months in your refrigerator
in an airtight container.
Marzipan:
Blend 1 cup almond paste with % cup light
corn syrup, 3 tsp. almond extract, and &
tsp. salt, Gradually beat in 1 pound of
powdered sugar. Knead portions with food
colors, roll out or shape as desired.
