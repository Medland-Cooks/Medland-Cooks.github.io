What is most appreciated by kids away at school?
Money, you say. Perhaps. But what they really crave is
food. Mom’s cooking. Something sweet. Spiced Dream
Bars fill the bill perfectly. The all-natural ingredients start
with coconut, nuts and brown sugar. The flavorful
difference is the warm, rich aroma .of allspice —the
single spice that tastes like a blend of flavors with cloves
the top note. Dream bars don’t need any refrigeration —
provided they’re wrapped tightly in foil—and they
travel beautifully.
SPICED DREAM BARS
1 cup sifted all-purpose flour
1-1/4 cups firmly-packed light brown sugar, divided
1/2 cup butter or margarine
2 eggs, well beaten
: 1 tablespoon flour -
1 teaspoon ground allspice
1 teaspoon baking powder
1/2 teaspoon salt
1 cup shredded coconut :
.1 cup chopped nuts
Preheat oven ‘to 350 F. In a large bowl combine 1 cup
flour with 1/2 cup of the brown sugar. Cut in the butter.
Mix to form a bdll, Turn into an ungreased 9-x 9-x 2-inch
baking pan. Pat uniformly over the bottom. Bake for 30
minutes. Meanwhile, combine remaining 3/4 cup sugar
with eggs, 1 tablespoon flour, allspice, baking powder and
salt. Stir in coconut and nuts. Spread over the baked
layer. Return to oven and bake until brown, 20 to 25
minutes. Cool in pans. Cut into bars. Yield: 24 bars.
