HAZELNUT MACAROONS
Bake at 350° for 15 minutes.
Makes about 114 dozen cookies.
2 egg whites
1 cup sifted 10X (confectioners’
powdered) sugar |
1 tablespoon lemon juice |
1 cup unblanched hazelnuts, ground
1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
18 whole unblanched hazelnuts
1. Beat egg whites until foamy-white in 2
~small bowl. Sprinkle with sugar and
lemon juice; continue beating until stiff
glossy peaks form. Fold together with
ground nuts and cinnamon,
2. Butter and flour a cooky sheet. Drop
- mixture onto' sheet, using two tea-
spoons; leave about 1 inch between
cookies. Set a whole hazelnut in the
center of each and let stand, uncovered,
for 5 to 7 hours in a warm room. '
3. Bake in moderate oven (350°) 15 to
20 minutes, or until golden brown. .
