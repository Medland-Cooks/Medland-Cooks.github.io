1. Cream butter or margarine with sugar
until light and fluffy in a large bowl.
Add almonds, almond extract, egg and
egg yolk and mix thoroughly. :

2. Gradually stir in flour until dough is
smooth enough to handle. Knead for a
minute or two, until smooth, on a light-
ly floured pastry board. Wrap in wax
paper and chill for 2 hours.

3. Using a generous amount of butter,
grease small fluted tart-shell pans.
Press an even layer of dough into each
pan, making sure that you press it firm-
ly into all the indentations. (Keep any
leftover dough chilled until you are
ready to bake it.)

4. Bake in moderate oven (375°) for
about 15 minutes, or until a pale
golden sand color. Invert over rack and
‘tap bottom of mold gently with a spoon

" or rub with a cool damp cloth until tart
slips out. Cool completely and serve
plain or filled with whipped cream and
a dab of jam or jelly.
