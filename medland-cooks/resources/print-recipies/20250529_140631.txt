MESS OF BEANS WITH HAMv
1 Ib. bag Great Northern beans
Ham bone or 14 1b. ham (preferably

not lean)
Freshly ground black pepper (as

much as you can stand)
1 large onion, chopped
Soak the beans in cold water to cov-
er overnight. Bring to a boil and add
the ham. Season heavily with black
pepper. Simmer for several hours,
till the beans begin to fall apart and
form a light gravy. Serve with raw
onion on the side for garnish.
