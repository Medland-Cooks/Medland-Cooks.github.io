Strawberry Shortcake
1/2 cup butter or margarine
1/2 cup sugar
I cup all-purpose flour sifted with
I teaspoon double-acting
baking powder
1/2 cup cornstarch
2 eggs, beaten
Cream butter or margarine with
sugar until light and soft. Sift the
flour and baking powder with the
cornstarch. Add beaten eggs to
creamed mixture, then fold in the
flour mixture with a metal spoon.
Place batter in two 7-inch round
lined layer cake pans and flatten on
top. Bake for 20 minutes in a
preheated 375 degree oven on the
top shelf.
Filling And Topping
1-1/2 cups fresh strawberries
2/3 cup whipping cream
1 egg white
sugar to taste
Prepare strawberries; whip cream
in one bowl and egg white in
another. Fold together and sweeten
if wished. Cover one layer of cake
with some of the strawberries and
cream. Top with second layer of
shortcake and the rest of the
strawberries and cream. To serve
cut in thin slices. Serves 6 to 8.
