RIZ AU FOIE DE VOLAILLE o
(Rice with chicken livers) '

3 thsp. butter, divided . 5
14 c. finely chopped onion '
1, tsp. finely minced garlic

2 chicken livers, cut into small dice
Salt, pepper to taste
1 ¢. raw rice ‘

1 bay leaf | '

114 cups chicken broth 18
Melt two tablespoons of the butter in
a saucepan and add the onion and
garlic. Cook briefly, stirring, until
onion is wilted. |

Add the chopped livers, salt and pep-
per. Cook, stirring, until the livers
lose their raw look. Add the rice and
bay leaf and stir. Add the broth,
bring to a boil and cover.
Cook over low heat exactly 17 min-
utes. Remove the bay leaf. Fluff the
rice with a fork and stir in the re-
maining tablespoon of butter. Yield:
Four servings,
