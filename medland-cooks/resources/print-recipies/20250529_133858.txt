Sift together dry ingredients. Cream butter and sugar well. Add egg and

sorghum or molasses. Beat. Blend in dry ingredients, mixing thoroughly. Roll

dough into balls the size of small walnuts. Roll balls in pearl sugar. Place on un-

greased cookie sheet. Bake at 375°F. for 9 to 12 minutes. Cookies will be crisp.

Makes about 4 dozen. 12/81
