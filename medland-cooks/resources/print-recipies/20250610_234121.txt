Makes about 6 dozen cookies.

5 eggs
214, cups sugar
15 cup ground blanched almonds
14 cup chopped citron
L4 cup chopped candied orange peel

S to 6 cups sifted all-purpose
flour, as needed
14 teaspoon each, ground

14 teaspoon each, ground cinnamon
and cloves (optional):
1 teaspoon baking powder

1. Beat eggs with sugar until thick and
pale yellow in a large bowl. Add al-
monds, orange peel and lemon rind.

2. Sift flour with spices and baking pow-
der and add gradually to egg mixture,
stirring well between additions. Add
enough flour to make a fairly stiff
dough that is not sticky. Knead on a
floured pastry board until smooth.

3. Shape into finger-thick rolls, then cut
in 1-inch slices. Arrange on a buttered
cooky sheet and let dry, uncovered, at
room temperature, overnight. |

4. Bake in slow oven (300°) for about
20 minutes, or until pale golden brown.
