IRISH SODA BREAD

4 c. sifted flour

1tsp. soda

1tsp. salt

4 tbsp. sugar :

2 tbsp. butter or margarine

1 egg, slightly beaten

1'% c. buttermilk

1c.raisins

Sift together flour, soda, salt and sugar.
Cut in butter with a pastry blender until
particles are fine.

Add egg to buttermilk and stir into dry
ingredients until flour is just moistened.
Add more buttermilk if needed to make a
soft dough.

Gently stir in raisins. A

Turn out on lightly floured board or counter
top and knead for a few seconds. Place in
a greased, round 8-inch cake pan. Cut a
cross on the top to prevent cracking.
Bake in a very hot oven (450 degrees) for
45 minutes or until loaf sounds hollow
when tapped with knuckles. Turn out on
wire rack to cool. Makes one loaf.
