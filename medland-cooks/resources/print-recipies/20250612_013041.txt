SAVORY LAMB KABOBS

1 pound lean lamb, cut into cubes

3 cup clear Italian dressing

/2 teaspoon oregano leaves

16 DROMEDARY Pitted Dates

4 DROMEDARY Whole Pimientos, drained,

quartered

1 large green pepper, cut into 16 pieces

Place first three ingredients in a bowl; cover and
marinate overnight in refrigerator. Drain, reserving
marinade. String on skewers alternating lamb,
dates, pimientos and green peppers. Brush with
some of marinade. Broil, approximately 5 inches
from source of heat, for 15 minutes or until done,
turning once and brushing with remaining marin-
ade. Makes 4 (1 kabob) servings.
