4 O’CLOCKS
1/, cup sugar
14, cup butter
1 egg, beaten
1 cup flour
1 tsp. soda
P 1/, tsp. salt :
1 tbsp. lemon juice
1 tsp. grated lemon peel
1/, cup flour
Cream together butter and sugar until very
light. Add beaten egg. Add lemon rind. Sift
together the cup of flour, soda, and salt, and
stir into the mixture. Stir in the lemon juice.
Then add up to 14 cup additional flour, beating
between additions. Put 14 cup sugar into a
bowl. Dip fingers into the sugar, pinch off a ball
of dough and roll it to the size of a walnut and
then dip the ball into the sugar. Arrange balls
on greased baking sheet three inches apart. Bake
at 375° for 12-15 minutes. This recipe makes
large flat cookies that spread as they bake. The
original directions said, ‘““Stamp each cooky with
a butter print in flour pattern to make the 4
oclock.
