Wedding Bells :*,'.-.‘a.,.
Popcorn: 2 quarts
Butter the insides of three custard cups and set aside.
Cook light sugar syrup and mix well with popped corn.
Press popcorn mixture firmly into custard cup, filling
it about half full and then lining the remaining portion
with one layer of popcorn to leave a hollow in the bot-
tom for the bell clapper. Cool and unmold onto waxed
paper. Insert white pipe cleaners or candy sticks into
tops of bells for hangars and decorate with ribbons.
For clapper, insert toothpicks into Maraschino cherries
or red candies and fasten to bottom of bell. To make
bells glistening white, cover with frosty icing.
