DATE STICKS
e i 4
1/ cup butter I/ tsp. baking powder 5
1 cup sugar /4 tsp. soda
1 egg 1 tsp. Schilling Cinnamon
1 tsp. Schilling pure Vanilla Extract /4 tsp. salt
2 cups sifted all-purpose flour 1 cup chopped dates
Cream butter; gradually add sugar. Cream thoroughly. Add egg and vanilla, beat-
ing well. Sift together flour, baking powder, soda, cinnamon and salt. Slowly add
the dry ingredients. Mix in dates. Shape cookies into thin sticks about 11/ inches
long. Bake on a greased cookie sheet 15 to 20 minutes in a 350° F. oven. Yield:
3 dozen.
