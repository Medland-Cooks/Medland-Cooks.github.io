HEARTS OF PALM SALAD

For each salad, cut chilled canned hearts of
palm into strips, then place a few on 1 medi-
um or 2 small romaine leaves on each salad
plate. Garnish with 2 small slices of stuffed
olives and 2 thin green-pepper rings, seeds re-
moved. Sprinkle lightly with chopped salted
nuts and paprika. Serve with French dressing
or mayonnaise, thinned with light cream.
