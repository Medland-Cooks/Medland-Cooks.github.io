BROCCOLI SALAD

2 bunches raw broccoli,

chopped fine
2 bunches green onions,

chopped fine
1 pint mayonnaise
juice of 1 lemon
1 basket cherry tomatoes, quartered

Mix all ingredients. Let stand
covered in refrigerator overnight.
(Keeps in refrigerator 5 days. The
broccoli bunches are the way they
are sold in markets. The onions are
about 10 to a bunch.) This recipe can
be cut in half.
M. Lucille Holmes
Minneapolis, MN
