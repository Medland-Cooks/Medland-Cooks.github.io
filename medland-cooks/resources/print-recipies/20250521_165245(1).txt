TOMATO-ASPIC SALAD
2 tablespoons gelatine | 1 cup cooked peas
14, cup cold water 2 small tomatoes, peeled
2 cups consomme 4 hard-cooked eggs
1 cup tomato juice 1 can asparagus tips
Dissolve gelatine in cold water about 5 minutes. Heat consomme and
add gelatine. Then add tomato juice, peas, diced tomatoes, and sliced
eggs. Place asparagus tips around outside of a mold, with tips down. Cut
off ends which extend above pan, and add to salad. Pour in a little gela-
tine mixture and place in main food compartment to congeal. This will
hold the asparagus in place. When congealed, remove and add remainder
of gelatine mixture. Return to refrigerator to congeal. Serve on endive
or lettuce. Tomatoes cut into petals and topped with grated hard-cooked
egg yolks make an attractive garnish. Serve with mayonnaise.
