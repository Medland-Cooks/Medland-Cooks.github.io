JAM 'N CRESCENT SWEET ROLLS
8-oz. can Pillsbury Refrigerated
Quick Crescent Dinner Rolls
2 tablespoons peach, pineapple
or apricot preserves or orange
marmalade
2/3 cup powdered sugar
14 teaspoon grated lemon peel
2 tablespoons preserves
1 teaspoon lemon juice
2 tablespoons finely chopped
nuts, if desired
Preheat oven to 400°. Lightly grease
cookie sheet. Separate crescent dough
info 4 rectangles; firmly press
perforations to seal. Spread 2 rectangles
with the 2 tablespoons preserves.
Place remaining 2 rectangles over
preserves, lightly press together. Cut
each rectangle lengthwise into 4 strips.
Twist each strip several times and form
into a coil on prepared cookie sheet.
Bake 10 to 13 minutes until golden
brown. Remove from pan immediately.
In small bowl, combine remaining
ingredients except nuts; spread over
rolls. Sprinkle with nuts. Serve warm.
8 rolls.
HIGH ALTITUDE: No change.
