FINNISH BREAD
FINSKA PINNAR
Called either ‘‘bread’ or ‘‘fingers,’” this
cooky is claimed by both Swedes and
Finns, and no Danish or Norwegian
cookbook would be complete without a
recipe for it.
Bake at 375° for 10 to 12 minutes. !
Makes about 3 dozen cookies. :
1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter or
margarine -
1/, cup sugar ‘
1 teaspoon vanilla or almond
, extract .
21/, cups sifted all-purpose flour
2 egg yolks beaten with 2 table-
| spoons cold water
1/, cup finely chopped blanched
almonds
| Sugar for sprinkling
1. Let butter or margarine soften slightly
at room temperature; then cream with
sugar until light and fluffy. Add flavor-
ing and gently mix in flour until just
blended, but not too smooth.
2. With lightly floured hands, roll dough
into long strips about the thickness of
a little finger. Cut into 2- to 3-inch
lengths and press top lightly with the
back of your finger. Brush with egg
yolk, sprinkle with almonds and sugar.
Place on unbuttered baking sheet.
3. Bake in moderate oven (375°) for 10
to 12 minutes, or until golden.
