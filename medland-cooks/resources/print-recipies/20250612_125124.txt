in half. On wax paper, shape each half into a

On baking day:

Start heating oven to 350°F. Remove cookie
rolls from refrigerator. Slice each, crosswise,
into slices, Ya-inch thick, Bake, on greased
cookie sheets, about 10 to 12 minutes or until
done. Cool on wire racks, then store in a con-
tainer which has loose-fitting cover. Makes
about 25.

To freeze: Make cookies as above; then freezer-

wrap and freeze up to 2 months. To serve, thaw,

unwrapped, at room temperature about 15 min-
utes.
