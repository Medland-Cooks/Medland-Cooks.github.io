HOT MINCEMEAT PIE WITH RUM SAuUCE

Make early on day or day bhefore:

1. Prepare 1 package piecrust mix as label
directs.

2. On lightly floured pastry board, with rolling
pin, roll out half of dough into a circle 11”
to 12” in diameter. Fold pastry circle in
half, lift onto ungreased 9-inch pie plate,
with fold at center; unfold; fit gently into
pie plate, then brush shell lightly with 1 egg
white. Refrigerate along with rest of pastry.

3. Start heating oven to 425°F. Now combine
32 cups prepared mincemeat with 12 cup
applesauce; mix well. Turn into pie shell;
trim bottom crust even with edge of pie
plate; moisten its edge with water. Roll out
rest of pastry into 12-inch circle; fold in
half, then with knife make several slits near
center so steam can escape; lay over fill-
ing. With scissors trim upper crust to 1 inch
beyond edge of plate; fold overhang under
edge of lower crust; press to pie-plate rim,
at 1%-inch intervals, with floured 4-tined
fork; or flute if preferred. :

4. Bake pie 30 to 35 minutes or until nicely
browned. Let cool, then refrigerate. Remove
20 minutes before serving, and warm up In
a 325°F. oven, Makes 8 servings.
