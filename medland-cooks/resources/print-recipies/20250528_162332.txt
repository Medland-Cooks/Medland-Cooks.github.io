ROAST BEEF
Eye of Round Roast
Salt and pepper
Preheat oven to 500°. Cook roast,
uncovered, five minutes per pound.
Turn oven off and leave roast one
and a half hours. DO NOT PEEK!
Slice. Serve hot for dinner, cold for
lunches.
