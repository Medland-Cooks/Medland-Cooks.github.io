HOBNAILS

1 cup light brown sugar

5 cup shortening

1 egg, beaten

114 cups sifted flour

14 tsp. soda

5 tsp. salt

1 tsp. cinnamon

1 tsp. vanilla

/5 cup raisins

Cream together the sugar and shortening,
add beaten egg and vanilla. Sift together the dry
ingredients, stir them in a little at a time, and
beat well. Fold in the raisins. Form into small
balls. Place on greased cookie sheet about three
inches apart. Bake at 375° for 12-15 minutes.
This recipe may also be made by substituting
15 cup chocolate bits for the raisins and omitting
the cinnamon.
