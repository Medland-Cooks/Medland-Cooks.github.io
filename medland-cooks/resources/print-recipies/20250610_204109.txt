| CARMEL BARS
Melt 40 carmel squares in double
boiler with 6 tblsp. Carnation milk
or I use regular milk.
Mix together:
1 cup oatmeal
1 cup flour
3% cup brown sugar
% tsp. soda
% tsp. salt
Add % cup melted butter. Press % of
mixture in bottom of 9 x 13 inch
pan. Bake at 350° for 10 minutes.
Sprinkle on 1 cup chocolate chips
and % cup chopped nuts.
Spread carmel mixture over top and
use remaining crumbs on top. Bake
for 15 minutes.
