ALMOND SPICE BREAD
BELGIUM—PAIN D' AMANDES

Bake at 375° for 10 minutes. '

Makes about 5 dozen cookies.

4 cups sifted all-purpose flour
1 scant teaspoon baking powder
1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
Pinch of salt
134 cups blanched almonds, grated
1 cup plus 2 tablespoons firmly
packed light brown sugar
14 cup brandy
15 cup milk
34 cup (114 sticks) unsalted butter
or margarine, melted
1. Sift flour with baking powder, cinna-
mon and salt. Stir in almonds and
sugar. Combine brandy, milk and but-
ter, and stir into flour mixture. Add
~ more flour, if necessary, to give a dough
that will pack together firmly.
2. Press into a roll, 114 inches thick; wrap
in wax paper and chill overnight.
3. Cut into V4-inch-thick slices. Arrange
on a lightly buttered cooky sheet.
4. Bake in moderate oven (375°) for 10
minutes. Cool on a rack.
