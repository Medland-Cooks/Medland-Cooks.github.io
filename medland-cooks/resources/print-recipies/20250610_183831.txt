BRICK OVEN PLUMB CAKE*
1 cup dark brown sugar
34 1b. butter or margarine
(114 sticks)

4 eggs, beaten

14 cup molasses

1 1b. seedless raisins

1 1b currants, plumped

14 1b. citron, chopped

114 tsps. cloves

114 tsps. cinnamon

1 tsp. mace or nutmeg

2 cups sifted flour

3 tbsps. sherry

3 tbsps. brandy

Cream butter, mix in sugar until light and
fluffty, add beaten eggs and molasses. Add
raisins, plumped currants, and citron. Sift the
flour with the cloves, cinnamon, and mace and
stir in. Mix thoroughly, then stir in the sherry
and brandy. Prepare three small loaf pans by
greasing them and lining with waxed paper or
foil. Spread batter in pans. Bake at 300° for
114 hours.
*Plumb cake was the old term for fruit cake.
Raisins were known as plumbs; hence the term.
