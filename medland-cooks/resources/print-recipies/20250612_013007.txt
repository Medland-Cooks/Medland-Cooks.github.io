MAPLE DATE MOUSSE
1 envelope unflavored gelatine
Ya cup cold water
3 eggs, separated
12 cup maple syrup
/s teaspoon salt
Few drops maple flavoring
1 cup DROMEDARY Pitted Dates, finely snipped
2 cup chopped pecans
Whipped cream, optional
Soften gelatine in cold water; stir over hot water
until dissolved. Remove from heat and set aside.
Beat egg yolks with next three ingredients until
thick and light colored; blend in gelatine mixture.
Beat egg whites until stiff. Fold into egg yolk mix-
ture with next two ingredients. Pour into a serving
dish. Chill thoroughly, at least 4 hours. Garnish
with whipped cream, if desired. Makes 6 (about 1
cup) servings.
