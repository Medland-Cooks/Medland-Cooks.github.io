Just mash six ounces of softened
cream cheese with a fork, then add
one-half cup mayonnaise. Blend
well. |

Stir in one-half cup pecans, one
cup salad olives, two tablespoons
olive juice and a dash of pepper —
NO salt. Mix well. |

The filling will be slightly mushy,
but after it is refrigerated for a few
hours, it will be firmer and of
spreading consistency.

‘Besides making a delicious sand-
wich, this is great on toast or let-
tuce leaves. Or, combine the two
(toast and lettuce leaves) if desired.
