APRICOT RHUBARB JAM/

8 c. finely chopped rhubarb ;
4 c. sugar

1 can apricot pie filling ‘

1 (3-0z.) pkg. orange-flavored

gelatin

Combine rhubarb and sugar in a
bowl (not metal). Allow to stand
overnight. In the morning [transfer
to a pan}, bring to a boil and simmer
10 minutes. Add the apricot pie fill-
ing and bring to a boil. Add the
gelatin and stir until dissolved. Put in
jars, cover and refrigerate or freeze. .
