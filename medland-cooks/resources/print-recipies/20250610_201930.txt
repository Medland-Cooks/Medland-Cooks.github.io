RECIPE NO. 233

DELICIOUS BEEF ON BUNS m%w

“A tasty snack for a group of 25” FOODS

1 (2%-0z.) envelope dry onion soup
7 Ibs. lean chuck roast
5 bouillon cubes
1 cup water
Brown meat in small amount of fat in Dutch oyen or heavy skillet. Add
remaining ingredients, cover and bake in 275° oven for 4 to 6 hours. The
meat must be very tender.
Remove meat from juice, cool. Slice very thin and return slices to juice. Add
extra water as needed to keep meat moist when it is reheated for serving.
Taste to check seasoning. Serve between buns with relishes and potato
chips.
