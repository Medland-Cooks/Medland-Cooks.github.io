CHRISTMAS BEADS

Any time before using:

In small bowl, dissolve a small amount of red
food-color paste in a little water. Dip 2 yards
butcher’s twine into this color bath, then stir
a few times until it is tinted an even red; then
remove twine from bowl, squeeze gently, let dry.
Any time up to one week before decorating tree:
1. Start heating oven to 200°F. Sift %2 cup

granulated sugar with Ys teaspoon cream of

tartar.

2. In small bowl, with electric mixer at high
speed, beat 2 egg whites (at room tempera-
ture) stiff enough to hold their shape. Sprin-
kle in sugar mixture, 1 tablespoon at a time,
beating until meringue forms stiff peaks.

3. Line a 15%” by 12” cookie sheet with foil.
Then dip point of knife into red paste, and,
with it, draw one thin line up the inside of a
pastry bag, that has tube number 22 in place,

' about three-fourths of its length.

4. Now fill pastry bag with meringue; then,
with it make swirls, l-inch wide, and 2-
inches apart, on a cookie sheet, about 4

| swirls to a row. Make 4 rows of swirls on
| cookie sheet; then lay tinted twine, very

" lightly, on swirls, continuing twine from one

| row to the second, to the third, and then to

: the last row. (Leave 5 inches of twine at be-

ginning and at end of chain, so you can tie

ends together.) Lastly, top the twine on each

meringue swirl with another meringue swirl.

5. Bake resulting beads 1% hours or until they

are dry. Makes a chain of 16 beads with
which to decorate your Christmas tree.

To vary: For small beads proceed as above, but

- make swirls of half size. Makes about 30.
