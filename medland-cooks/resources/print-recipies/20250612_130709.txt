PECAN PIE -

Make day or two before, if desired:

1. Prepare unbaked 9-inch pie shell, folding
overhang of piecrust under. Press about 18
pecan halves into piecrust edge at Y-inch
intervals. Then, with- floured 4-tined fork,
press edge of piecrust to pie plate between
every two pecan halves; as pictured. Refrig-
erate.

2. Start heating oven to 325°F. In saucepan
combine 1 cup granuiated sugar, 2 tea-
spoon salt, 1 cup dark corn syrup; simmer
until sugar dissolves. Meanwhile, beat 3
eggs foamy.

3. Now, into syrup, stir 2 cup butter or marga-
rine, 1Y2 teaspoons vanilla, 2 cups coarsely
chopped pecans, then eggs; turn intg\;@,,,
shell. Bake 40 minutes; cool; refrigerate.
Serve with or without 1 cup heavy or whip-
ping cream, whipped. Makes 1 9-inch pie.
