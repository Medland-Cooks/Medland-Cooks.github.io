MAPLE ROCKS

1 cup maple sugar

145 cup butter or margarine

2 eggs, beaten

15 cup rich milk or thin cream

2 cups flour

1 tsp. soda

14 tsp. salt

This recipe was made years ago with hard
sugar pounded or grated, but soft maple sugar
may also be used. Cream together the sugar
and butter. It is not necessary to get out all
the lumps. Add the beaten eggs and milk or
cream. Beat well. Sift the flour with the soda
and salt and stir into first mixture. A little
more flour may be needed to make the dough
stiff enough to roll. Roll out on floured board
14 inch thick. Cut in small circles. Place on
greased cookie sheet about three inches apart.
Bake at 375° for 10-12 minutes. When cool,
frost with maple frosting (bottom of p. 13).
