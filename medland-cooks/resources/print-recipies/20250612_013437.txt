Blend first two ingredients in large mixing bowl.
Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each
addition. Combine next four ingredients; gradu-
ally stir into egg mixture with vanilla extract. Fold
in remaining ingredients. Spoon into greased and
floured 9x5x3-inch loaf pan. Bake in a preheated
slow oven (325°F.) about 1 hour or until done.
Cool in pan 30 minutes (do not invert). Remove
from pan and cool on wire rack. Makes 1 (9-inch)
loaf.
