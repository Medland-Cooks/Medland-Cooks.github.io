LOLLISTICK FAVORS:

Early on the day, make these enchanting table
favors for the Children’s Christmas Party. For
each, remove paper from a cone or rocket
shaped lollipop;* trim its stick to about 1%”.
Now decorate it, as pictured, by brushing lolli-
pop surface with a little white corn syrup, then
sprinkling with multicolored sprinkles, dragées
or confectioners’ sugar. Then gently insert each
lollipop stick into center of a Sugarplum Puff.
*You may write Don Rudolph Enterprises, Den-
ver, Colorado, about purchasing these.
