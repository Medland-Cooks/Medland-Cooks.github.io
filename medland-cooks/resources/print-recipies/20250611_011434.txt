CHICKEN DIJON

A touch of hot mustard makes a subtle

difference in this broiled chicken.

Preparation time: 10 minutes.

Marinating time: 2 hours.

Makes 6 servings at 19¢ each.

2 broiler-fryers (about 12 pounds
each)

34 cup vegetable oil

3, cup tarragon vinegar

3 tablespoons hot prepared mustard

1 teaspoon seasoned salt

15 teaspoon seasoned pepper

1. Remove giblets and necks from chicken
packages and save to make chicken
broth. Cut chickens into serving-size
pieces.

2. Combine oil, vinegar, mustard, salt
and pepper in a large bowl; add
chicken. Marinate 2 hours, turning
once, in refrigerator.

3. Place chicken, skin side down, on
broiler rack.

4. Broil, 6 inches from heat, 15 minutes;
turn. Broil 20 to 30 minutes longer,
basting frequently, or until chicken is
tender, | °
