RECIPE NO. 354
LORRAINE’S ENGLISH TOFFEE @ )
"candy lovers will appreciate this recipe" FOODS
1 Ib. butter 1 cup (3 oz. pkg.) slivered
2 cups sugar almonds
5 Tbsp water 6 plain Hersey candy bars
2 tsp vanilla about half a 3 V4 oz. pkg. chop-
2 tsp salt ped pecans
In a heavy saucepan, melt butter slowly. Add sugar, water, vanilla,
salt. and almonds. Blend well. Place on hot fire and stir constantly un-
til almonds and syrup are dark golden brown (to 300° on candy ther-
mometer). Pour onto buttered cookie sheet. Let cool, cut while still
warm, lay Hersey candy bars on top. Spread when melted. Top with
chopped pecans. Can be kept at room temperature.
