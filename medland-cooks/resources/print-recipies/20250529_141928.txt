Birthday Cake Wﬂ
Popcorn: & quarts
For two white layers: Grease a 9-inch layer cake pan
and set aside. Cook light sugar syrup, pour in fine
stream over two quarts of popped corn and mix well.
Press mixture evenly into pan. Remove from pan
immediately onto waxed paper. Repeat procedure for
second layer.
For one red layer: Repeat above method, adding 1 tea-
spoon (or as desired) of red food coloring to cooked '
syrup. When mixed with popcorn, press into pan.
Unmold the red layer while still warm, place it be-
tween two white layers and press together firmly.
Decorate the cake with small American flags. Cut in
wedges to serve, dipping knife frequently into hot
water while slicing.
