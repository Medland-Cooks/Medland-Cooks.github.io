BELLYLAPS
PENNSYLVANIA DUTCH
These are similar to the MORAVIAN
- BROWN SUGAR COOKIES (recipe, page
92), and a little less brittle, so they
can be used for tree ornaments.
Bake at 350° for 10 to 15 minutes.
Makes about 3 dozen cookles.
2 cups golden molasses
13 cup unsalted butter or margarine,
melted
1 egg, well beaten
4 cups sifted all-purpose flour
2 teaspoons baking soda
2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
1/, teaspoon ground nutmeg
1. Mix molasses with butter or margarine
and the beaten egg. Sift flour twice with
baking soda and spices. Resift into
molasses mixture, adding just enough
flour to make a dough you can roll.
