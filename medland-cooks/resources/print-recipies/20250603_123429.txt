HOT POTATO, BACON AND FRANK SALAD Serves 6
Y2 \b. bacon, diced 3 pounds potatoes, cooked, peeled
1 \arge onion, chopped and sliced
Y2 cup red wine vinegar 1 cup sliced celery
1 Ib. frankfurters or knockwurst, Salt and pepper
sliced 3 hard-cooked eggs, sieved
Fry bacon until crisp. Remove crisp pieces. Add onion to bacon drippings and saute
until golden. Carefully and slowly pour in vinegar. Add franks, potatoes and celery.
Stir gently until very hot. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Sprinkle with sieved
egg and pieces of crisp bacon. Serve hot. |
