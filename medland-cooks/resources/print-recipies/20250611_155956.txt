MIX-IN-THE-PAN BROWNIES*
Fudgy-rich and so easy to make right in
the pan.

Preparation time: 15 minutes.

Bake at 350° for 30 minutes.

Makes 16 brownies (1Y2 pounds) at 4¢

each.

%3 cup vegetable shortening

1 cup sugar

2 eggs

1 teaspoon vanilla

1 cup sifted all-purpose flour

> teaspoon baking powder

Y2 teaspoon salt

Y2 cup dry cocoa

Y2 cup chopped walnuts

1. Melt shortening in 8x8x2-inch baking
pan over low heat. Stir in sugar. Cool.

2. Break eggs into a small bowl; add
vanilla; beat with fork until blended.
Stir into sugar mixture in baking pan.

3. Measure flour, baking powder, salt
and cocoa into sifter. Sift into mixture
in baking pan. Mix thoroughly with
fork. Add walnuts. Smooth top.

4. Bake in a moderate oven (350°) 30
minutes, or until center springs back
when lightly pressed with fingertip.
Cool on wire rack. Cut into 16 squares.
Decorate with frosting and green gum-
drop leaves, if you wish.
