MOLASSES COCOANUT CHEWS
1 cup white sugar
1 cup brown sugar
1 cup shortening
2 eggs, beaten
2 tsps. vanilla
14 cup molasses
4 cups sifted flour
1 tsp. salt
114 tsps. soda
1 cup cocoanut flakes
Cream together the white and brown sugar
and the shortening. Add the beaten eggs, vanilla,
and molasses. Beat well. Sift together the flour,
soda, and salt and stir in. Stir in cocoanut flakes.
Shape into small balls and place on greased
cookie sheet about three inches apart. Bake at
375° for 12-15 minutes.
