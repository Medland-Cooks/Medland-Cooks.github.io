Sponge Cake
4 eggs
2/3 cup sugar
I cup all-purpose flour
2 tablespoons hot water
Combine eggs and sugar in a
large mixing bowl, and beat until
the mixture is thick and creamy.
Sift the flour. Fold gently into the
egg mixture with a metal spoon;
last fold in the water. Grease the
pan lightly and coat with flour or
an equal mixture of flour and sugar.
Spoon batter carefully into a 7-inch
spring form pan and bake 20
minutes at 375 degreeson arackin
the middle position of the oven.
Cool 1 minute in pan and remove
to cooling rack. To serve split and
fill with jam or cream. Makes 10 or
12 servings.
