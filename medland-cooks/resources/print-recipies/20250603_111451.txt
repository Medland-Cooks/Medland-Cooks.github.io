REFRIGERATOR CUCUMBERS

7 cups sliced unpeeled cucumbers

1 cup cut up green peppers

1 cup cut up onions

1 tablespoon salt

Mix these ingredients and let set

three hours.

Mix 2 cups sugar

1 cup apple cider vinegar

1 teaspoon celery seed

1 teaspoon celery salt

Wash and slice cucumbers. Do not

peel. Add peppers, onions and salt.

Mix well and let set three hours.

Drain. Mix the sugar, vinegar and
~ spices, but do not boil. Stir often un-

til sugar dissolves. Pour over drained

cucumbers. Put in jars and put in re-

frigerator. Do Not Freeze. These

keep well in refrigerator for months.

Use as needed.

Eleanor France

Wausau, Wisconsin
