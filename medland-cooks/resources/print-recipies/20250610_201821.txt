RECIPE NO. 411
CREAMY FRUIT SALAD W

“a variety of fruits, whipped cream, and a tapioca base” FOODS
1 (3%-0z.) pkg. vanilla tapioca pudding
1 (16-0z.) can fruit cocktail, drained-reserve juice
1 (8-0z.) can pineapple chunks, drained-reserve juice
1 (11-0z.) can mandarin oranges, drained-reserve juice
2 pint whipping cream
1 cup miniature marshmallows
4 bananas, sliced
Prepare pudding mix as directed, substituting mixed fruit juices for milk.
Cool thoroughly. Whip cream; blend with pudding, canned fruits and
marshmallows. Add bananas just before serving. If added earlier, dip in
lemon juice to prevent darkening. Serves 14to 15
Other fruits or chopped nuts may be added.
