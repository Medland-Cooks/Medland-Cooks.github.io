ULTIMATE BLUEBERRY PIE

8 oz. cream cheese

% c. sugar

1 tsp. vanilla or lemon juice

% c. heavy cream

1 c. blueberry jam

1% c. whipping cream

1 tbsp. sugar

2 c. fresh blueberries, washed and

drained ;

Bake 10-inch pie crust. Cool. Beat
together cream cheese, sugar, vanilla or
lemon juice and heavy cream until it
reaches a fairly thick pudding texture.
Pour into cooled crust. Spread jam on top
of cream mixture. Chill. Beat whipping
cream until stiff and fluffy. Add sugar. Add
blueberries. Mound in pie shell. Chill.
(Recipe developed by Deborah Shubat, a
graduate student in the department of
horticultural science at the University of
Minnesota.)
