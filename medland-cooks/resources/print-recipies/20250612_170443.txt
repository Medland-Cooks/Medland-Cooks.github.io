Light Popcorn Balls
1 c. sugar 1 tbsp. butter or margarine
1 c. corn syrup 3 qts. salted popped corn
1/ c. water 15 nutmeats, about
Combine sugar, corn syrup and water in saucepan.
Cook over medium heat, stirring, until sugar dissolves.
Continue cooking without stirring, to 250°F. or until
a little of the mixture dropped in cold water forms a
hard ball. Remove from heat. Stir in butter. Pour
syrup over corn, stirring, until kernels are coated. Let
cool a few seconds. Quickly shape into 3-inch balls,
placing a nutmeat in the center of each. Yield: about
15 popcorn balls. ;

Combine molasses, corn syrup and vinegar in saucepan.
Cook over medium heat, stirring to 270°F. or until a

brittle. Remove from heat. Stir in butter. Pour syrup
over corn, stirring until kernels are coated. Let corn
cool a few seconds. Shape into 2%-inch balls. Yield:
about 10 popcorn balls. ‘ :
