HEAVENLY FRUIT CAKE
4 eggs 1 teaspoon ground
1Y3 cups light brown nutmeg
sugar, firmly packed 2 (8-ounce) jars mixed
2 teaspoons vanilla candied fruit
extract 1 (6'2-ounce) jar red
6 tablespoons butter candied cherries,
or margarine, halved
softened 2 (8-ounce) packages
1%, cups sifted DROMEDARY
all-purpose flour - Pitted Dates,
1 teaspoon baking snipped or
powder DROMEDARY
1 teaspoon salt Chopped Dates
1 teaspoon ground 4 cups coarsely
allspice chopped pecans
Beat eggs until light and fluffy. Add next three
ingredients and beat until fluffy. Sift together
next five ingredients. Stir into egg mixture until
smooth; then stir in remaining ingredients. Turn
into a well greased 9-inch tube pan spreading
batter evenly. Bake in a preheated slow oven
(325°F.) for 2V4 hours, or until done. Cool in
pan on rack for 30 minutes; then remove to rack
to cool completely. To store, wrap in aluminum
foil and refrigerate.* Keeps well several weeks.
Makes 26 (about 1-inch) slices.
*If desired, wrap cake in cheese cloth saturated
with brandy before wrapping in foil.
