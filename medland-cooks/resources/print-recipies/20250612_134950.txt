NUT-FILLED CAKES

Make early on day:

1. In small bowl, with a fork, combine 1%a
cups finely-ground California walnuts with
/3 cup white corn syrup, 1 teaspoon rose
water and 1 tablespoon butter or margarine,
until thoroughly blended; refrigerate this
filling.

2. Start heating oven to 350°F. In medium
bowl, with spoon, combine 2% cups sifted
regular all-purpose flour, 12 tablespoons
granulated sugar, 3% cup melted butter or
margarine and 1 tablespoon milk until mix-
ture forms a ball. Then knead very well.

3. Now, for each patty, pat 12 teaspoon of
this dough between palms of hands until
about 1% inches across; lay on ungreased
cookie sheet. Next top each of half of pat-
ties with Y% teaspoon nut filling; then top
each with a second patty, pressing edges
together with a fork.

4. Bake about 12 to 15 minutes, or until a
light golden. Cool, then store, tightly cov-
ered, until serving time. Then sprinkle gen-
erously with confectioners’ sugar, before ar-
ranging on cake plate. Makes about 5 dozen.
