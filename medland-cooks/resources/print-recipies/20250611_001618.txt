Makes about 5 dozen cookies.
14 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
or margarine '
34 cup sugar
1 whole egg
1 egg yolk
12/ cups sifted all-purpose flour
1/, teaspoon baking soda
1> teaspoon ground nutmeg
5 cup coarsely chopped walnuts
1/, cup currants or raisins
Ground nutmeg and sugar for
sprinkling

1. Cream butter with sugar until light and
flufty in 2 medium-size bowl. Beat in
whole egg and yolk.

2. Sift flour with baking soda and nutmeg.
Mix into batter. Fold in nuts and
raisins.

3. Drop from a teaspoon, 2 inches apart,
onto a buttered cooky sheet.

4, Bake in moderate oven (375°) for 10
to 12 minutes. Remove from cooky
sheet while warm. Cool on rack. Store
in an airtight container.
